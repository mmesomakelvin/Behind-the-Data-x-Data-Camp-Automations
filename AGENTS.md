# Repository Working Instructions

## Communication Style

- Always explain work in plain, everyday English.
- Clearly say what we are building, why it is needed, and what it will do.
- Avoid technical terms when simpler words will work.
- When a technical term is necessary, explain its meaning immediately in simple language.
- Give the user clear, practical next steps without assuming technical knowledge.
- Keep explanations concise, but include important risks, decisions, and anything the user must do.
